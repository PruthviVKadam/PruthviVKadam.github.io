Recommenders Live

Recsys Studio

  • Problem: Most portfolio recommenders report RMSE on a random split — which rewards a number nobody ranks by and leaks the future into the past.
  • Approach: Implicit-feedback recommenders (popularity → item-kNN → ALS) scored by NDCG/Recall/MAP on a leakage-free global temporal split, with catalog coverage and novelty.
  • Result: The popularity baseline wins raw accuracy (NDCG@10 0.124); ALS trades it for 7.5× the catalog coverage — baselines are hard to beat, shown honestly.
