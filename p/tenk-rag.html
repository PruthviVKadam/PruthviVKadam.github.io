Problem

Answering questions over dense SEC filings needs grounded, cited answers — and a way to measure retrieval, which most RAG demos never show.

Approach

Item-aware chunking of real 10-Ks → ChromaDB with ONNX all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (no torch) → answers that cite their Item; Groq when keyed, else an extractive cited fallback.

Result

Every answer shows its sources beside it, plus a reproducible retrieval eval over a labeled question set.

Insights

What it shows

Grounding is non-negotiable. The UI never shows an answer without its cited sections — a hallucination becomes obvious instead of plausible.

Retrieval is the part to measure. Hit-rate / recall / MRR over a labeled set is what separates "a RAG demo" from a system you can trust.

No-key by default. The extractive cited fallback keeps the app fully usable without an LLM key — and avoids quoting generation metrics that weren't actually run.
