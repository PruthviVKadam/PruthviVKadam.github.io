Problem

Default-risk models have to be explainable and tied to a business threshold — a black-box probability nobody can challenge won't pass a credit committee.

Approach

XGBoost on the UCI credit-default data (11 curated features) behind a FastAPI service, with native TreeSHAP per-decision contributions and a movable cost-based cutoff.

Result

A scoring API where every decision comes with a "why", and where you can watch approval / recall / precision trade off as you move the threshold.

Insights

What it shows

Explainability is a product feature, not an afterthought. Per-decision SHAP turns a score into a defensible answer to "why was I declined?"

The threshold is a business choice, not a default 0.5. The explorer makes the approval / recall / precision trade-off explicit so risk and growth can negotiate it.

Ship it like software. A FastAPI + Docker service with a frozen golden-prediction test is deployable and regression-safe — not a notebook.
