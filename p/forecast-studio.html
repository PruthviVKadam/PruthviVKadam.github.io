Problem

Forecasting is full of traps — leakage, invalid cross-validation, and fancy models that quietly lose to a naive baseline nobody bothered to run.

Approach

A Plotly Dash app benchmarking ARIMA, Prophet and XGBoost-lags vs naive + seasonal-naive baselines, using an expanding-window walk-forward engine that raises if train overlaps test.

Result

A leaderboard (MASE / MAPE / RMSE) you can trust — where the baselines are always present and the honest takeaway is often "they're hard to beat".

Insights

What it shows

Leakage is the failure mode. Models are factories re-fit per fold; nothing fit on the future. The backtest raises if a split overlaps — correctness enforced in code.

Always ship the baseline. ARIMA edges naive by a hair and XGBoost loses outright — a leaderboard without baselines would have told a flattering lie.

MASE < 1 is the literacy bar. It's the honest "did we beat naive?" test, reported per model and ranked.
