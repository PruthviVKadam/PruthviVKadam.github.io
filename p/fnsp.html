Open the live demo → View code

Problem

News→stock demos are usually notebooks that secretly peek at the future, then report a fantastic backtest. The hard part isn't the model — it's not lying.

Approach

Bucket news by its known-at timestamp and prices by bar close; score sentiment (VADER, FinBERT optional) → features → a model trained with purged walk-forward CV against price-only and naive baselines.

Result

A terminal where a real leakage test drives the feature pipeline, beats_baseline is read from a committed evaluation, and the backtest ships with buy-and-hold + random-entry controls.

Insights

What it shows

Leakage is the whole game. A real test drives the actual feature pipeline and proves a future article can't influence an earlier row — the discipline most "news→stock" demos skip.

Modest beats honest. Markets are near-random, so AUC 0.616 over a 0.524 price-only baseline is a small, real edge — reported as such, not dressed up.

The backtest tells on itself. The on-screen Sharpe is flagged as an in-sample illustration on a low-noise synthetic series, shown beside buy-and-hold and random-entry controls — the comparison is the point, not the absolute return.

Validated sentiment, with a disclaimer. The scorer is measured on a labeled benchmark, and a "not financial advice" notice ships on every surface and API payload.
