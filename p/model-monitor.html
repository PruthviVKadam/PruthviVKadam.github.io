Problem

Models rot silently — inputs drift and the scored population changes, but teams find out from a business metric weeks later.

Approach

Stream batches through PSI / KS / Jensen-Shannon drift + prediction-drift + performance tracking over the live Credit Risk model, then inject a known covariate shift and time the detector.

Result

A monitor that flags the shift fast and cleanly — and an honest story about when accuracy doesn't see it coming.

Insights

What it shows

AUC can be blind to the shift that matters. Accuracy held steady while the scored population's real default rate doubled — input drift caught what the headline metric missed.

Detection is measurable. Injecting a known shift turns "we have monitoring" into "latency 2 batches, 0% false alarms" — a number you can hold a system to.

I killed my own hypothesis. An early "calibration degrades" story didn't survive the data, so I rewrote it to the AUC-blind truth. Honesty over a tidier narrative.
